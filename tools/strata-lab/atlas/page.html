Strata Atlas

Worlds made the way Strata makes them, as maps: about 100 km of land lived through three and a half million years. Plates push up ranges and pull open rifts, rivers wear the land down and branch out on their own, ice ages gouge valleys that fill as lakes, dry basins end in salt. The climate runs across the map, so one world holds several, and every place gets one of Strata's 65 biomes. Every day has its own world. Drag across the map to cut a section through it.
