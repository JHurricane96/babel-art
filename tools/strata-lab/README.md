# Strata lab

Tools for developing Strata, a new artwork (see the vision doc linked from the
session). Write outputs to a scratch folder, not into the repository.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `viewer/` | A self-contained HTML viewer for the land: regions generated and lit in web workers, drawn at a whole number of screen pixels per art pixel (4 by default, as on the TVs) so it looks crisp on any monitor. Pan, zoom, change seeds, jump between biome edges, drift, and mask to the four-screen wall. **Journey** (prototype) sends a party of four along the regions' routes (`engine/strata/route.ts`) with the camera following, at 1×, 10× or 50×; **Route** shows the route. Ladders, ropes, bridges and dug tunnels and shafts are drawn on the overlay where the route needs them; nothing is built into the world yet. Build with `node tools/strata-lab/viewer/build.mjs OUT.html --standalone`. |
| `world/` | The world overview: builds one world's history (`engine/strata/history.ts`: a finite world stepped through 48 eras of uplift, folding, rifting, volcanoes, climate, rivers, erosion and deposition) and draws it: climate along the world, the whole world in cross-section with a draggable close-up, crossing rivers and faults, and an era slider that replays history. Build with `node tools/strata-lab/world/build.mjs OUT.html --standalone`. |
| `atlas/` | **Strata Atlas**: worlds as maps, from `engine/strata/world2d.ts` (Strata's history in two dimensions: about 100 km of land over sixty eras. The map lies across six to eleven drifting plates: where they press together ranges rise, some old and worn down to hills, some young; where they pull apart rifts sink with lakes along them; some plates are sea floor, so coasts come with bays, inlets and islands; volcanic fields grow their cones and granite domes come to light. Rivers cut down by the stream-power law and branch out by themselves; two or three ice ages send glaciers down the valleys, gouging them and leaving lakes behind moraines; in dry basins water ends in salt flats and salt lakes. The climate is a game world's: every world holds the whole range in a patchwork of some seventy regions about 10 km across (frozen, cold and wet, mild, hot and dry, hot and wet, each several times, and some odd ones), blended through wandering borders, with warmth falling with height and rain wrung out over the mountains on one wind; so ice, pine forest, temperate woods, steppe, desert and jungle all lie within a day's walk, and the days differ in where they lie and in rarer things (volcanoes, salt basins, granite domes), and every place gets its habitat, soil and one of the 65 biomes of `biomes.ts`). Today's world (its seed is the date) and five others, made in workers; biome, climate, height, rain, warmth and rock views; what is under the pointer; a section along a line dragged across the map, showing the strata folded under the ranges. Build with `node tools/strata-lab/atlas/build.mjs OUT.html --standalone`. |
| `vista/` | **Strata Vista**: today's generated world (`engine/strata/world2d.ts`, seeded by the date, made in a worker) at the wall's resolution (980 x 637 art pixels) on the GPU (WebGL2). `land.ts` turns the 200 m world map into land at any point: smooth heights with relief by how rugged it is, rivers traced down the drainage and cut as channels, lakes and the sea at their levels, the biome (borders wandering), what kind of ground that biome makes, and which trees grow there. Places chosen for what lies within a walk (several biomes, a rise and fall of the land, water, rock; one of each kind of climate first, then the best well apart; a Go to menu), each with a walk of a few km planned across the land (A* by slope, round water) the way that crosses the most biomes and height, from its low end; in the land, mesas and buttes in the dry country, crags and tors elsewhere, and on rugged ground benches with cliffs where the rock's beds show; the walked line hidden (Path shows it) and kept clear of trees and tall plants; a map of the whole world. Terrain in quadtree patches from three grids (a 2 m and a 16 m grid laid over the place, a 100 m grid over the whole world) built in workers; the ground takes its biome's grass, leaf and sand colours; ground shaded in world-space texels at least an art pixel across, on six-shade ramps; rock faces show their beds; haze in eight bands of distance; houses as real geometry with pixel-art walls and roofs (none in generated worlds yet); every sprite drawn at its exact height in whole pixels (`pack.ts`, made in the workers): trees, shrubs and rocks from 6 px up by whole rows and columns of one drawing, rows in proportion to height (`grow.ts`, tables the shaders read), so each size is the one below with a row or column more and nothing changes its look as it grows; grass, flowers and ferns redrawn at each size (strokes); trees and shrubs near the camera taken apart into groups of leaf clumps, each put back at its own place; anything taller than its tallest drawing drawn in pixels two or three times the size, so it keeps growing; ground cover out to 1.4 km, where even a shrub has shrunk to a pixel and faded by its size (beyond 560 m only what stands 0.6 m or more), made a few tiles a frame; drawn in materials and shades, so no two trees are coloured alike (16 leaf colours a kind, now and then a tree turning). Speeds up to 250x, a slider along the route, 10 and 100 m steps and frame stepping. Camera shots: Follow (looks ahead into the land, turning again where the way turns away), Trail (close behind the party, looking the way they go) and Turn; Auto follows, and drops into Trail while trees or a hill would hide the party (tested against the trees and the land between), whole-pixel camera snapping, a window through things that stand in front of the party, and a frame-time and GPU readout. Build with `node tools/strata-lab/vista/build.mjs OUT.html --standalone`; `window.vista.hold()` and `.step(dt)` drive it frame by frame for checks under software GL. |
| `lookbook.ts` | Renders a fixed set of places (one per landform and the key biomes) as viewer-like stills in Node, several processes at a time, into contact sheets; `--before DIR` renders another checkout too and pairs each place. Each still is checked automatically (streaks in the rock, very dark masses, ground pressed flat at the sky's ceiling, framing) and flagged stills get a red frame. 20 places take about 7 s. `node --import tsx tools/strata-lab/lookbook.ts OUT_DIR [--before DIR] [--only 1,5] [--spots FILE]`. |
| `probe.ts` | Everything that made one place, layer by layer: the world's column, landform, climate, habitat, soil and biome, ground and water, rock beds, the generated column as runs of material, and the renderer's depth and light; `--png` writes a zoomed crop. `node --import tsx tools/strata-lab/probe.ts SEED X [Y] [--png OUT.png]`. |
| `world-cache.ts` | A disk cache of world histories (under `node_modules/.cache/strata-worlds`, keyed to the engine's code) for the lookbook, the probe and the generation tests. |
| `land-render.ts` | The lab's land renderer (sky, strata, cave light), shared by the viewer and `regions.ts`. |
| `regions.ts` | Renders land from the region generator (`engine/strata/generate.ts`): a four-region panorama, wall-sized close-ups of each region edge, and a 12-seed contact sheet (skip it with `--quick`). Prints generation time, scaled to the mini PC. |
| `pi-budget/` | Estimates frame cost on the target machine from this one (`--target 780m` for the Ryzen 7 / Radeon 780M mini PC, `pi5` for a Raspberry Pi 5): CPU time scaled by a calibration factor from published benchmarks, GPU time from a cost model. Spikes for the material simulation and the light flood. See its README. |
| `sketches/motion.py` | A 20-second motion test of the cavern panel, with light options (stepped, steady, calm, banded, smooth) and a count of pixels the light alone changes per frame. |
| `sketches/mockup.py` | A static mock-up of one dusk tableau on the default four-screen wall, used to settle the look: pixel size, light, palette. Needs numpy and Pillow. |

```sh
node --import tsx tools/strata-lab/regions.ts /tmp/strata-land 7 --quick   # seed 7
python tools/strata-lab/sketches/mockup.py /tmp/strata 320 480 640   # pixel sizes
python tools/strata-lab/sketches/mockup.py /tmp/strata --variants   # looks at 480
```

The look variants (`VARIANTS` in the script) are a dark cut-away with pools of
light, a brighter illustrated cross-section, smooth instead of stepped light,
a warm sandstone day, and a cold granite night.

Each size is the pixel-art width of one 531 mm panel (480 = 4×4 screen pixels
per art pixel on a 1080p panel). It writes the full wall at screen resolution,
a 1600-pixel preview, and a 1:1 crop around the camp.

Tests: `npm run test:strata` (about 12 s with the world cache warm) or `npm run test:strata:quick` (determinism, seams, water, budget: about 6 s).
