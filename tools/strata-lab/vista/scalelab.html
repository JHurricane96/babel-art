Sprite Growth Lab

One scene and one camera at the wall's pixel size, and six ways for trees to change size as it moves. Trees in clumps takes trees apart into the leaf clumps, trunk and branches they were drawn from and puts each back at its exact place, so a tree grows evenly instead of jerking in blocks. Everything does each kind of thing its own way: clumps for trees and shrubs, and grass, ferns, rocks and people redrawn at their exact size. Watch the trees as the camera moves in, across, turns and moves back.
